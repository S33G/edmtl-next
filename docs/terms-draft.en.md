## About our services

Entretien Domestique Montreal (EDMTL) provides property cleaning and maintenance services in Montreal and the surrounding area. Contact us to discuss the work you need and your property.

## Free, no-obligation quotes

Our quotes are free. Requesting a quote does not oblige you to book a service. The form sends us your inquiry; it does not confirm an appointment.

## Your service request

Tell us which services you are interested in, your postal code and how to reach you so we can discuss your project. The work details and price are to be confirmed with EDMTL.

## Service area

We serve Montreal and areas including Saint-Lazare, Laval, the North Shore, the South Shore and Vaudreuil-Dorion. Travel farther afield may be available at a quoted rate.

## Payment methods

We accept major credit cards and e-transfer. The payment arrangements for your project are to be confirmed when booking.
